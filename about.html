About

WICKED
STRAYS.

A Japan-based independent animation and story studio creating original works for the world.

What is it?

A studio, not a single story.

WICKED STRAYS exists to develop and produce original animation and stories. Projects can be short films, series, experimental pieces, music videos or something that does not fit a familiar format.

Philosophy

Our principles.

Equality

Everyone deserves to be treated with respect. A job title does not make someone more valuable as a person.

Freedom

Creators should have room to make things in ways that suit them.

Respect

Different opinions are welcome. Creative disagreement is not a personal attack.

Well-being

Production should not require people to destroy their health, sleep or personal lives.

Ownership

Creator rights and accurate credits matter. The studio should grow works with creators.

Originality

We want to make things audiences have not seen before.
