CURRENTLY UNPAID

WICKED STRAYS is still in its early stages, and we currently do not have enough funding for production.

Because of this, some collaboration opportunities at this stage will be unpaid.

We want to be transparent about this before anyone joins us. Participation is completely voluntary, and you should only join if you are comfortable with these conditions.

What we expect

Create with respect.

01 / COMMUNICATION

Keep communication clear and let the team know about your availability and limitations.

02 / RESPECT

Respect other creators, their ideas, boundaries and contributions.

03 / RESPONSIBILITY

Do your best to meet agreed deadlines and responsibilities.

04 / VOLUNTARY PARTICIPATION

No one is expected to participate in every project. Choose the level of involvement that works for you.

Creator Rights

Your work matters.

We want creators to receive proper credit for their contributions.

Ownership, usage rights, credits and compensation will be discussed and clarified for each project whenever possible.

Please do not contribute work if you are uncomfortable with the agreed terms.
